The process

Three steps, start to payout

You bring what you remember. We bring the fund registries, the paperwork, and the follow-through.

01 - TELL US

Tell us your story

Your name, your work history, whatever details you remember - even fragments. There's no minimum to start.

02 - WE TRACE

We trace the fund

We search fund administrators, the FSCA's unclaimed benefits registers, and old employer records under your name.

03 - WE CLAIM

We handle the claim

Once a fund is confirmed, we prepare and submit the claim and keep you updated until the money is paid out.

Why Benyte

Built for people who've lost track, not people who never had any

Funds go unclaimed for ordinary reasons: an employer that folded, a fund that merged, an address that changed. Benyte exists to close that gap between a member and money that was always theirs.

  • →No fee to start a trace - we're only paid once a fund is confirmed and you agree to proceed.
  • →We deal directly with fund administrators and the relevant regulatory registers on your behalf.
  • →Every claim is tracked with a reference number, from first trace to final payout.
  • →You keep control - nothing is submitted to a fund without your sign-off.
