The process

Three steps, start to payout

You bring what you remember. We bring the fund registries, the paperwork, and the follow-through.

01

Tell us your story

Your name, your work history, whatever details you have — even fragments. There's no minimum amount of information to start.

02

We trace the fund

We search fund administrators, the FSCA's unclaimed benefits registers, and old employer records to locate money registered under your name.

03

We handle the claim

Once a fund is confirmed, we prepare and submit the claim on your behalf and keep you updated until the money is paid out.

Why Benyte

Built for people who've lost track, not people who never had any

Funds go unclaimed for ordinary reasons: an employer that folded, a fund that merged, an address that changed. Benyte exists to close that gap between a member and money that was always theirs.

We work directly with fund administrators and the relevant regulatory registers, so you're not the one making cold calls to a call centre that's changed its name twice since you last worked there.

  • →You keep control — nothing is submitted to a fund without your sign-off.
